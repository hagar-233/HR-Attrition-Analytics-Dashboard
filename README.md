## 💡 Key Business Insights

* **High Overall Attrition:** Overall turnover stands at **33.44%**, which is significantly higher than standard industry benchmarks (10–15%).
* **Retention Paradox:** Despite a strong satisfaction score (**3.89/5**) and high performance ratings, employee exit rates remain high due to competitive market offers.
* **Primary Exit Drivers:** The top reasons cited for leaving are seeking external opportunities (*Another Position*), dissatisfaction (*Unhappy*), and competitive compensation (*More Money*).
* **High-Risk Demographics:** Peak turnover is concentrated in two main age groups: **>70** (retirement transitions) and **30–40** (career advancement stage).
* **Production Department Vulnerability:** The Production department accounts for the highest headcount (209 employees), yet suffers from a high **29.54%** attrition rate.
* **Recruitment Channel Performance:** Online Web Applications exhibit the highest turnover rate, whereas **Employee Referrals** demonstrate the highest retention efficiency.
* **Managerial Impact:** Attrition rates cluster heavily under specific leadership, reaching up to **61.90%** within certain management teams.
